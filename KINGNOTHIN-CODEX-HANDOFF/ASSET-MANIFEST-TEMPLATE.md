# Asset Manifest

Copy rows for every meaningful site asset. This prevents Codex from mistaking old experiments for approved material.

| File/path | Type | Used by | Status | Source/license | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/example.ext` | image/font/video/audio | section or component | approved/experimental/obsolete/missing | origin and usage rights | crop, sequence number, replacement, etc. |

Status meanings:

- **approved:** may be used and preserved.
- **experimental:** show Steve before promoting into the live experience.
- **obsolete:** retain only if history is needed; do not use in current designs.
- **missing:** referenced or needed but not presently available.
