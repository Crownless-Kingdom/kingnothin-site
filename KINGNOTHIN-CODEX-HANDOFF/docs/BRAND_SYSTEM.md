# KINGNOTHIN Brand System

## Character

The brand is controlled, ominous, elegant, and legible. It combines neo-brutalist mass with art-nouveau precision: obsidian structure, living jade circuitry, restrained gold filigree, flashes of crimson, and future-city geometry.

References are principles, not imitation:

- Stanley Kubrick: deliberate framing, symmetry, pacing, negative space, and unease.
- Truman Capote: precision, economy, observation, and clean verbal rhythm.

## Palette roles

- **Obsidian black:** dominant field, weight, silence, and institutional mass.
- **Jade green:** data, life, circuitry, sovereignty, awakening, and constructive signal.
- **Crimson red:** extraction, warning, consequence, opposition, and the Ledger.
- **Supernova gold:** rare authority, sacred detail, discovery, and emphasis.
- **Regal silver:** machinery, interface structure, secondary type, and neutral detail.
- **Ivory white:** highly legible primary text and moments of human clarity.

Exact color values must be centralized as CSS custom properties. When integrating with an existing site, retain its approved tokens.

## Typography

Approved families:

- Dosis
- Exo
- Prototype
- GC Epic Pro Demo

Do not introduce Jost or Space Mono. Do not silently substitute a “similar” font. Use sensible fallbacks only while an approved local font fails to load, and report the missing asset.

## Composition

- Symmetry is a brand conviction, not a default template.
- Center headings and major statements.
- Keep major headings and subheadings on a single line when space and readability allow.
- A deliberate two-line composition is acceptable when it looks stronger or prevents cramped text.
- Use responsive type scaling rather than clipping, microscopic type, or uncontrolled wrapping.
- Maintain generous negative space and clear visual hierarchy.

## Motion

Motion should communicate a system changing state: decrypting, waking, transmitting, assembling, extracting, or reclaiming. Avoid decorative motion with no narrative job.

- The primary tagline decrypts when entering view and may re-encrypt after leaving the viewport.
- Canvas may support particles and generative effects.
- Preserve smoothness on ordinary devices; pause or simplify offscreen effects.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences with an immediate, readable state.

## Avoid

- Generic dashboard-card grids as the dominant identity.
- Stock cyberpunk neon, random glitch, illegible code rain, or decoration masquerading as meaning.
- Excessive gradients, glow, animation, or copy that competes with the central signal.
- Corporate cheerfulness, startup clichés, fantasy-monarchy worship, or conventional luxury branding.
